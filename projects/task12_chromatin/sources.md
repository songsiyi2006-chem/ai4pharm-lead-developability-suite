Every biological numerical parameter is hypothetical. Concentrations use nM, association constants nM^-1 h^-1, first-order rates h^-1, and penalties kcal mol^-1. RT = 0.61633 kcal mol^-1 approximates 310 K. The energy penalty controls a declared thermodynamic scenario, not a measured nucleosomal free energy. The project contains no experimental nucleosome occupancy dataset and is not a calibrated BRD4, EZH2 or HDAC predictor.
