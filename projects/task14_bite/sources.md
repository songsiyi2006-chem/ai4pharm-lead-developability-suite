# Task 14 primary sources and evidence register

Verified online 2026-09-21.

| Source | Supported observation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Betts et al., 2019, CD3 bispecific QSP model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6531394/) | Trimer formation can be linked mechanistically to target-cell killing. | Our rates and receptor counts are not fitted from this paper. |
| [T-cell engagers: model interrogation..., 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11493665/) | Antigen abundance and asymmetric affinity affect trimer dose-response shape. | A hook in a binding model does not imply a universal clinical downturn above 1 µM. |
| [Population dynamics of immunological synapse formation..., 2023](https://elifesciences.org/articles/83659) | Cell-level synapse assembly depends on interacting effector and target populations. | Our coarse-grained molecular contact volume is not a cell-pair population model or measured synapse geometry. |

All receptor copy numbers, affinity values, effective contact volume, E:T ratios, Hill coefficients and lysis rates are illustrative scenario assumptions. Avogadro's constant is exact. No patient efficacy, cytokine release threshold, clinical self-inhibition exposure or dosing recommendation is calculated. The BiTE concentration is externally maintained during lysis; antibody PK is outside this module.
