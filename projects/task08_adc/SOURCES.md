## Parameter policy

Every key in [config.json](config.json) is registered in [parameter_provenance.csv](parameter_provenance.csv). The Avogadro constant is the exact SI value 6.02214076×10²³ mol⁻¹. Structural capacity is four interchain disulfide bonds and up to eight thiols, as required by the task. All kinetic, chromatographic, permeability, geometry and pharmacodynamic values are either task-specified modeling conditions or explicitly hypothetical/numerical settings. A task-specified number is not an experimentally measured parameter.

The high and low scenarios use the same antibody trafficking, proteolysis, diffusion, metabolism and PD assumptions; only the permeability constant changes from 0.2 to 0.002 h⁻¹. This is a controlled mathematical comparison, not an estimate of the difference between DXd and MMAF. Their actual linker chemistry and released species also differ. In particular, the model's generic effective Val-Cit-like cleavage must not be presented as the exact linker chemistry of trastuzumab deruxtecan.

Reports summarize source findings in original wording; publisher figures, tables and full texts are not redistributed. All archived CSV trajectories are generated by this project's code.
