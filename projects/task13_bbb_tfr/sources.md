# Task 13 primary sources and evidence register

Verified online 2026-09-21.

| Source | Supported observation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Yu et al., 2011, DOI 10.1126/scitranslmed.3002230](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21613623/) | In the studied mouse system, reducing anti-TfR affinity increased parenchymal antibody uptake; high-affinity variants were retained at the BBB. | This does not establish a universal 50–500 nM optimum or human brain PK. |
| [Bien-Ly et al., 2014, DOI 10.1084/jem.20131660](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3920563/) | TfR trafficking and degradation can affect brain antibody delivery. | Our conserved-receptor model recycles the receptor after cargo degradation; it omits regulated receptor downregulation. |

All model volumes, amounts, binding and trafficking rates, initial concentrations, clearance constants and pH sensitivity coefficients are assumptions. Endosomal pH is a common time-dependent compartment approximation, not measured endosome age distributions. The model has no endogenous transferrin, avidity, efflux pump, tissue target binding, inflammatory BBB disruption, or clinical calibration. The AUC ratio is explicitly limited to 0–72 h.
